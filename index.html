Player Requirements

  • • Active Discord account with working microphone.
  • • Consistent availability for scheduled scrims and HRL league matches.
  • • Respectful attitude toward teammates, opponents, and staff.
  • • Willingness to receive constructive VOD review feedback.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I try out?
Join our Discord server using the button above, go to the tickets channel, and open a Tryouts Ticket.
What if a main team spot is full?
You may be placed in the Academy division until an active spot opens on Elite or Major.
What regions do you play?
We primarily compete in EU and NA Rocket League events and HRL leagues.
